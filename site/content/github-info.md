## Recent updates worth watching

- **Issue statuses and repository boards (public preview):** Issues can now carry more specific statuses than open or closed, and repository boards visualize them. This gives teams and agents shared context on what is in flight. Source: [GitHub Changelog, Nov 23, 2026](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-23-issue-statuses-and-repository-boards-are-in-public-preview).
- **More predictable notifications on GitHub.com:** Starting December 1, 2026, routine activity such as commenting or reviewing no longer resubscribes you to a thread you manually unsubscribed from. Worth knowing if you filter or manage notifications. Source: [GitHub Changelog, Nov 24, 2026](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com).
